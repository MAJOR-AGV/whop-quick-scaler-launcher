# Product page copy

By Major · Unofficial

Derived from [Sales page](../../skills/whop-quick-scaler-launcher/references/sales-page.md). Reference date: 2026-09-29. Draft from the exact approved offer and real deliverable. A neighboring product's inclusions or testimonials do not apply automatically.

## Produce the page, not just advice

Give the user a descriptive title, one concrete headline, a useful description, purchase-area wording and a short FAQ. Start with what the customer receives and the task it helps them do. Do not invent financial outcomes, speed claims, credentials, scarcity or testimonials.

The product headline has an 80-character maximum in the reviewed [Update Product](https://docs.whop.com/api-reference/beta/products/update-product.md) schema, checked 2026-09-29. Count the proposed text with a tool when available, including spaces and punctuation. If no counter is available, keep it short and label the exact count unchecked.

Copy-ready structure:

```text
[Product name]
For [buyer] who needs to [specific job].
You receive [verified deliverable] to use for [concrete task].

Included: [confirmed contents and format].
How access works: [actual steps and delivery timing].
Access period: [defined duration].
Payment: [approved due-now amount, currency and later billing].
Support: [approved scope and channel].
Not included: [important exclusions].
Purchase action: [label matching the actual checkout].
```

This is a template. Replace all bracketed fields before calling it publishable. Keep unresolved commercial or legal decisions outside the sales-copy block.

Answer the actual buying questions: who it fits, what is included, how access works, duration, billing, support, cancellation and refunds. Reconcile FAQ, gallery text, description and checkout terms. A saved description does not prove separate FAQ records changed.

## Media and buyer checks

Show the actual product, a readable excerpt or a clearly labeled demonstration. Use approved media rights, irreversible redaction and a crop suited to the current destination. Confirm the actual gallery and banner previews; a working design ratio is not a universal upload rule.

Keep essential purchase terms in readable page text. A generated receipt, person or performance chart cannot stand in for customer proof. Do not upload paid or private source files as public gallery assets.

Verify the correct seller, offer, loaded media, mobile layout and canonical checkout independently. Configured access, a loaded page, Marketplace discovery, payment and actual delivery are separate states. The advisory bot provides this checklist without claiming to have performed private account checks.
