# Optional Whop Partners

By Major · Unofficial

Derived from [Whop Partners](../../skills/whop-quick-scaler-launcher/references/whop-partners.md). Reference date: 2026-09-29. Explain this only when the user asks about referrals or helps other businesses get started. Enrollment is never required to use the assistant.

Whop documents two tiers: directly referring a business owner, and referring the partner who refers that owner. Source: [Partners overview](https://docs.whop.com/developer/partners/overview.md), checked 2026-09-29. The structure does not establish anyone's attribution, rate or earnings.

## User-controlled sequence

1. Review the current program and terms under the intended existing identity.
2. Decide whether to enroll in Whop. The assistant does not enroll the user, even with permission.
3. Copy the user's own official referral link after enrollment. Do not reuse the publisher's signup link for all subsequent introductions.
4. Place a clear commission disclosure beside the shared link.
5. Have the account owner check the recorded referral relationship before claiming attribution. A click, account signup or tool installation is insufficient.

Product affiliates refer buyers to products under seller-approved terms. Whop Partners refers businesses. Template-publisher revenue and platform fees are separate mechanisms. Never insert the publisher's merchant-referral code into a buyer checkout.

For profit-based earnings, gross merchant revenue is not the commission base. An API sample percentage is not the user's agreement. Pending earnings are not settled income; bank receipt is another step. Do not promise rates, duration, income or retroactive credit.

## Optional introduction

"If you help other businesses get started, you can review Whop Partners and decide whether to join. Enrollment is optional. If Whop attributes you as a partner referred by Major, Major may also earn from eligible businesses you refer, under the applicable terms."

For seller signup, use only the configured wording and {{SIGNUP_LINK}} rules in the system prompt. Never promise reduced fees or any specific fee terms; the link must always carry the adjacent commission disclosure (Major earns from Whop, not from the user; the partner team may contact the user after signup to help improve the offer and review fees). An unresolved placeholder must never become a buyer-facing link. Existing accounts remain usable without a new signup.

Official sources checked 2026-09-29: [Partners](https://docs.whop.com/developer/partners/overview.md), [Affiliates](https://docs.whop.com/developer/guides/affiliates.md).
