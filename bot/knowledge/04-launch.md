# Preparing a launch

By Major · Unofficial

Derived from [Launch playbook](../../skills/whop-quick-scaler-launcher/references/launch-playbook.md). Reference date: 2026-09-29. This is a recommended work sequence, not a promised review time, ranking or sales result. The assistant drafts the plan and messages; it does not publish or schedule them.

## Before choosing the date

Freeze the offer, copy, delivery route, tested package where relevant, demonstration, FAQ and exact links. Identify required destinations and their admission conditions. A simultaneous launch means public usability on the agreed date and timezone, not merely submitting every listing that day.

If a required destination needs a public release before review, resolve that dependency before announcing a common date. Private prereview is an option only when the destination actually supports it. Postpone or obtain an explicit scope change if the required same-day condition is impossible.

Keep new preparation hidden and closed to enrollment. Preserve unrelated live offers. Verify the canonical checkout independently; hiding a store option does not close its direct link.

## Recommended first-week sequence

- Day 1: After separate release approval, make the canonical resource available, then dependent formats. Check every required destination before the broad announcement.
- Day 2: Test the served package or buyer access route, not just the private build.
- Day 3: Check identity, version and link drift on owned listings and discovered copies.
- Day 4: Explain the existing demonstration with a useful, truthful use case.
- Day 5: Correct the most consequential verified setup defect.
- Day 6: Read actual counters with source, date, period and unit.
- Day 7: Close the review with one next improvement and its acceptance check.

Ask for one useful audience action per message: watch, try, report a problem or voluntarily support the project. Never buy engagement, invent testimonials or require a positive review. Stars, installs, conversations and sales are different measurements.

The receipt separates submission, acceptance, direct access, discovery, checkout availability and actual delivery. If a required destination fails after another is live, pause broad promotion and report the missed condition without deleting working resources or inventing success.
