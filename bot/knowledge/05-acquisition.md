# Content and product affiliation

By Major · Unofficial

Derived from [Acquisition](../../skills/whop-quick-scaler-launcher/references/acquisition.md). Reference date: 2026-09-29. This is a cross-tool editorial method, not a claim that Whop natively publishes articles, newsletters and social posts for the user. The assistant prepares drafts only.

## Article production

Choose a real reader question and check existing articles, service pages and active drafts before creating another. Approve the angle, source set, offer boundary and useful next action. Missing keyword data stays unmeasured.

Use a researcher, writer, visual producer, independent verifier and publisher. Keep durable handoffs: brief, claim-to-source notes, article, metadata, final assets, review verdict and publication receipt. Pause on access failures without discarding finished stages. Give one operator control of the publishing surface.

Every material claim needs the exact supporting page. Review the final image's text and numbers against the article; a diagram cannot introduce a claim the text does not support. Generated faces, receipts or balances are not customer evidence.

After separately approved publication, verify the exact served article, media, metadata, internal links and CTA. Sitemap inclusion can support discovery checks but does not establish search indexing, traffic or sales.

## Newsletter and social derivatives

Use the approved source. Clear inherited titles, links, images and schedules from duplicated drafts. Preserve conditional blocks and inspect email and web versions separately. Confirm audience, exclusions and consent before proposing a send.

Adapt each social draft to its audience, language and format. Deliver the final text, media, alt text, destination and first comment where needed. A date in a draft does not authorize scheduling. Avoid identical reposts and unsupported best-time claims.

## Product affiliation

Keep seller product affiliates separate from Whop Partners. Inspect effective global, member and custom settings; obtain owner approval for the commission base, scope, renewal behavior and reversal terms. Do not quote a historical default as the user's rate.

Preserve the native affiliate destination and any required checkout behavior. A link click is not proof of an attributed sale or settled commission. Put a disclosure beside the recommendation and never inject the tool publisher's merchant-referral code into buyer checkouts.
