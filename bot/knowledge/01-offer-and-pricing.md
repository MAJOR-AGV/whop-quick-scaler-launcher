# Offer and billing decisions

By Major · Unofficial

Derived from [Offer and pricing](../../skills/whop-quick-scaler-launcher/references/offer-and-pricing.md). Reference date: 2026-09-29. This assistant prepares a proposal; the user applies approved decisions in Whop.

## Start with delivery

Identify the buyer's job, the usable deliverable, format, inclusions, exclusions, delivery steps, support and access period. Use evidence of demand where available. A course creator's worksheet is a generic example, not a case study or proof of sales.

Choose one-time billing for a finished unit, recurring billing for a continuing benefit, or fixed installments for an approved finite commitment. Same access with different billing periods may use pricing variants of the same product; different entitlements require a deliberate product-access map.

Keep amount due now, setup fee, renewal amount, billing interval, total installment commitment and access expiry separate. Do not promise lifetime access: the reviewed [Seller Terms](https://whop.com/seller-terms/) require defined access periods and prohibit indefinite-access listings, despite older wording in the general pricing guide. Checked 2026-09-29.

## Trial, price and optional purchase checks

Whop documents trials for recurring links. Verify any initial fee, eligibility, first charge and cancellation route before writing "free today" or "no card required." A returning buyer may not receive another trial.

A proposed price must account for delivery, review, support, applicable fees and commissions, taxes and refunds. Unknown costs remain unknown. Calculate using tools or provide an explicitly uncomputed formula. Never treat a hypothetical fee as Whop's actual fee.

For an optional add-on, preserve access to the base purchase. Use a separate checkout, an obvious skip route and a final delivery destination. A stored redirect is configuration evidence, not proof of payment or delivery. Check displayed savings separately from the payable amount and use only a genuine approved comparison price.
